General Specifications

TT36 and TT48
Manufacturer Eastey Enterprises, Inc.
Equipment Rotary accumulation table
Models Covered TT36 and TT48
Installed Lines Tortilla Lines 1, 3, and 4
Turntable Diameter TT36: 36 in. (91.4 cm); TT48: 48 in. (122 cm)
Accumulation Area TT36: 7.07 ft² (0.656 m²); TT48: 12.57 ft² (1.168 m²)
Table Height 27.9–36 in. (70.9–91.5 cm)
Overall Height 30.9–39 in. (78.5–99.1 cm)
Base Size 30.1 in. long × 28 in. wide (76.5 × 71.1 cm)
Weight TT36: 260 lb (118 kg); TT48: 340 lb (154 kg)
Variable Speed Up to 13 revolutions per minute
Electrical Requirements 115 V or 220 V, 5 A, single phase, grounded
Construction Industrial-strength tubular steel frame, powder-coated steel base, and 7-gauge stainless-steel turntable
Direction of Rotation Reversible clockwise or counterclockwise operation
Sidewall Adjustable 5 in. (12.7 cm) circumferential sidewall
⚠️
Rotating and electrical hazards

Keep hands away from the rotating tabletop, support wheels, chain, sprockets, and sidewall supports. Disconnect electrical power before clearing jams, making adjustments, or performing maintenance.
