General Specifications

Lines 1, 3 and 4
Manufacturer Wexxar Packaging Inc. / BEL
Models BEL 505 and BEL 505S
Equipment Type Semi-automatic case former and packing station
Installed Lines Tortilla Lines 1, 3 and 4
Power Source Compressed air
Standard Air Pressure 80–90 PSI
Air Consumption 0.1 cubic feet per cycle
Main Function Folds the bottom major and minor case flaps and holds the erected case for packing
⚠️
Pneumatic safety

Shut off and disconnect the machine from its compressed-air supply before performing maintenance, service, or setup adjustments. Push the emergency-stop button before connecting an air supply.
